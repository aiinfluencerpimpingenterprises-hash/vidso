Help

Questions, answered

What is Vidso?

Vidso is an AI studio for long-form YouTube. Enter a topic and it generates a script, voiceover, B-roll, and captions into a full video you can export and upload. Built for creators who want to monetize with consistent long-form output.

How does AI long-form generation work?

You pick a topic and length. Vidso writes the script, generates narration, fetches matching B-roll, syncs captions, and renders a 16:9 MP4 you can preview, edit, and download.

What kinds of videos should I make to monetize?

Faceless explainers, listicles, story docs, and videos on niched topics tend to perform well: searchable topics with strong hooks and enough length to build watch time toward YouTube monetization.

How do I stay consistent for monetization?

Connect your channel to keep publishing consistently. Vidso helps you produce the next long-form upload faster so you can hit posting cadence, watch-hour goals, and revenue milestones without stalling.
