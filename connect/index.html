Set up in a minute

Pick your app. You sign in with your TubeOps account once, and your agent works with your plan's credits and channels.

  1. In Claude (web or desktop), open Settings → Connectors and click Add custom connector.
  2. Name it TubeOps and paste this link: 
    https://www.tubeops.pro/mcp
  3. Click Connect and sign in to TubeOps. That's it: ask Claude to make a video.

Opening the link in a browser brings you to this page. It only works when you paste it into your AI app.

  1. Run this in your terminal: 
    claude mcp add --transport http tubeops https://www.tubeops.pro/mcp
  2. Start claude, type /mcp, pick tubeops and sign in to TubeOps in the browser window that opens.
  3. Ask for a video in plain words. Claude Code runs the whole pipeline and tells you when the MP4 is ready.
  1. Open Cursor Settings → MCP and click Add new MCP server, or edit ~/.cursor/mcp.json:

  2. Click Login next to tubeops and sign in to TubeOps.

Questions

What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard AI apps use to call outside tools. Adding the TubeOps link gives your agent the same video tools you have in the TubeOps app.

Do I need a TubeOps plan?

Your agent works with your TubeOps account, so it uses your plan's credits, video minutes and channels, the same as making the video in the app.

Will my agent post to YouTube without asking?

It only uploads when you ask it to, to a channel you've connected in TubeOps. You can tell it to upload as private or schedule the video for later.

Can I still edit what the agent made?

Yes. Every video your agent makes shows up in TubeOps, where you can redraw scenes, change the pacing, add music and restyle the captions before you export.

I opened the link and saw this page. Is something wrong?

No. The MCP link is meant for AI apps. Paste it into your app's connector settings, using the steps above.
