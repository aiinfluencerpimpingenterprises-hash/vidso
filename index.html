Vidso makes long-form YouTube videos

Vidso is an AI production studio for faceless YouTube channels. Enter a topic and it writes a script, generates voiceover, assembles B-roll, burns in captions, and exports an MP4 you can publish.

What you can do in Vidso

  • Generate long-form YouTube videos from a topic or script.
  • Connect a YouTube channel in Account settings to upload finished videos.
  • Use the same YouTube connection from MCP clients after you sign in to Vidso.

Google account access

Signing in with Google creates your Vidso account (name and email). Connecting YouTube is a separate step. We only request YouTube access when you click YouTube MCP / Connect YouTube, and we use it to upload and manage videos on the channel you choose. Details are in the privacy policy.
